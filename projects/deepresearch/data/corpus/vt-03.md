The Velmora Tunnel cost 6.2 billion crowns to build, according to the final audit published by the Halden Rail Authority. That is 1.4 billion crowns more than the 2011 estimate. Auditors blamed most of the overrun on flooding in the eastern bore in 2015. Track fees are expected to repay the cost of the tunnel by 2051.
