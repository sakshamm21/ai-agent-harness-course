title: Seawater floods eastern bore of strait tunnel
source: Northbay Gazette
date: 2015-03-22
url: corpus://northbay-gazette/seawater-floods-eastern-bore-of-strait-tunnel
---
Work on the Velmora Tunnel stopped for eleven weeks after seawater flooded the eastern bore in March 2015. Nobody was hurt, but two boring machines were damaged. Engineers sealed the leak by freezing the surrounding rock with liquid nitrogen. The delay pushed the planned opening back by a year.
