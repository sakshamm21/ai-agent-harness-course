source: Nordic Earth Observation Agency
date: 2019-08-08

Satellites can measure soil moisture by listening to faint microwave signals from the ground. Wet soil gives off a weaker signal than dry soil. The measurements help farmers and flood forecasters. Several agencies plan new missions this decade.
