title: Orbis-7 antenna deploys on second try
source: Space Systems Review
date: 2021-03-19
url: corpus://space-systems-review/orbis-7-antenna-deploys-on-second-try
---
The mesh antenna on Orbis-7 opened fully on 18 March 2021, on the second attempt. A stuck latch had stopped the first attempt halfway. Controllers freed it by warming the latch in sunlight for a day. The antenna is the largest ever flown by the agency.
