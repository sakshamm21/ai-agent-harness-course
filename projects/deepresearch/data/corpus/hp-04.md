title: Storm damages lighthouse cottages
source: Northbay Gazette
date: 2009-01-27
url: corpus://northbay-gazette/storm-damages-lighthouse-cottages
---
A winter storm tore part of the roof off the keepers' cottages at Harrow Point in January 2009. The museum closed for six months while repairs were made. The tower itself was not damaged. Volunteers raised 120,000 crowns for the work.
