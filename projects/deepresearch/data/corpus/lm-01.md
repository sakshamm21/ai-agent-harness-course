title: What caused the Lake Mirren bloom
source: Wend Environment Agency
date: 2022-10-05
url: corpus://wend-environment-agency/what-caused-the-lake-mirren-bloom
---
The 2022 algae bloom in Lake Mirren was caused by phosphorus runoff from farms along the Tull River. A hot, still August let the algae multiply quickly. The agency measured phosphorus at three times the safe level near the river mouth. Similar but smaller blooms were seen in 2018 and 2020.
