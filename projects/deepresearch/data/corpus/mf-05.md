title: Counting the cost of Merrow fever
source: Wend Farming News
date: 2022-11-28
url: corpus://wend-farming-news/counting-the-cost-of-merrow-fever
---
Merrow fever cost sheep farmers in the region about 9 million crowns in 2021. Most of the losses came from dead lambs and lower wool sales. Some farmers have switched to cattle. Insurance for the disease is expensive and rarely bought.
