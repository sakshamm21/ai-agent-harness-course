title: Velmora Tunnel opens to passenger trains
source: Northbay Gazette
date: 2019-06-14

---
The Velmora Tunnel opened to passenger trains on 14 June 2019, linking Port Halden with the island city of Esker. The first public train left Port Halden at 06:10 and reached Esker 19 minutes later. Crowds gathered at both portals for the opening ceremony. Ferry operators on the strait expect to lose about a third of their passengers within two years.
