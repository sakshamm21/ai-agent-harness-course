title: Talon-2 rocket record year
source: Space Systems Review
date: 2021-12-30
url: corpus://space-systems-review/talon-2-rocket-record-year
---
The Talon-2 rocket flew nine times in 2021 without a failure. Its payloads included weather satellites and a lunar test probe. Each launch costs about 60 million crowns. A larger Talon-3 is planned for 2025.
