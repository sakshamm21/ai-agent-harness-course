title: Tessaly coffee exports recover
source: Tessaly Agricultural Journal
date: 2023-03-01
url: corpus://tessaly-agricultural-journal/tessaly-coffee-exports-recover
---
Tessaly exported 92,000 tonnes of coffee in 2022, the most since 2017. Officials credited the spread of rust-resistant trees. The biggest buyers were roasters in Lisk and Esker. Export prices rose by 6 percent over the year.
