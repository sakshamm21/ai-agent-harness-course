title: Kestrel Ridge to add battery storage
source: Coastal Energy Weekly
date: 2023-03-29
url: corpus://coastal-energy-weekly/kestrel-ridge-to-add-battery-storage
---
The operator of the Kestrel Ridge wind farm plans to add a 50-megawatt battery next to the substation. The battery will store power on windy nights and release it during the morning peak. Construction is due to finish in 2025. It will be the largest grid battery on the Wend coast.
