title: Rail fares across the strait
source: Halden Rail Authority
date: 2023-01-02
url: corpus://halden-rail-authority/rail-fares-across-the-strait
---
A single rail ticket from Port Halden to Esker costs 14 crowns in 2023. Season tickets are cheaper for daily commuters. About 38,000 people cross the strait by train on a weekday. Fares rise by 3 percent each January.
