The Corrin Bay desalination plant produces 120,000 cubic metres of drinking water each day. That covers about 40 percent of the water used in the city of Corrin. The plant takes in seawater through a pipe that reaches 1.2 kilometres out into the bay. It uses reverse osmosis membranes.
