title: Birds and blades: Kestrel Ridge study
source: Wend Nature Review
date: 2021-05-14
url: corpus://wend-nature-review/birds-and-blades-kestrel-ridge-study
---
A three-year study counted 62 bird deaths at the Kestrel Ridge wind farm, most of them gulls. The farm now pauses its turbines for two weeks during the spring kestrel migration. Researchers said the pause cut deaths by about half. The study was funded by the regional wildlife fund.
