title: Coffee rust spreads in the Ombe lowlands
source: Tessaly Daily
date: 2023-08-09
url: corpus://tessaly-daily/coffee-rust-spreads-in-the-ombe-lowlands
---
Leaf rust has reached coffee farms in the Ombe lowlands, where Sundew is rarely planted. Lowland farmers mostly grow the older Lowgrove variety. The agriculture ministry has started handing out Sundew seedlings in the area. Officials hope to replace a third of lowland trees within five years.
