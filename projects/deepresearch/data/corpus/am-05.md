title: Sodium-ion versus lithium-ion for home storage
source: Battery Tech Digest
date: 2023-04-22
url: corpus://battery-tech-digest/sodium-ion-versus-lithium-ion-for-home-storage
---
Sodium-ion cells are heavier than lithium-ion cells that store the same energy. For a battery that sits in a garage, weight matters less than price and safety. Sodium-ion cells are also harder to set on fire. Several makers, including Corvane Cells, now sell sodium-ion home storage units.
