title: What Orbis-7 will measure
source: Nordic Earth Observation Agency
date: 2020-11-18

---
Orbis-7 is built to measure sea surface salinity and soil moisture from space. Its main instrument is a microwave radiometer with a 6-metre mesh antenna. Salinity maps help ocean scientists track currents, and soil moisture maps help forecast droughts. The satellite covers the whole globe every three days.
