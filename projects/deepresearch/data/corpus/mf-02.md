title: New Merrow vaccine passes field trials
source: Wend Veterinary Bulletin
date: 2023-10-02
url: corpus://wend-veterinary-bulletin/new-merrow-vaccine-passes-field-trials
---
In field trials the new vaccine was 91 percent effective at preventing Merrow fever. The trials covered 12,000 sheep on 60 farms over two grazing seasons. Side effects were mild and lasted less than two days. The vaccine is given as a single injection before the tick season.
