title: Saltmarsh offshore wind farm approved
source: Coastal Energy Weekly
date: 2023-06-11
url: corpus://coastal-energy-weekly/saltmarsh-offshore-wind-farm-approved
---
The Saltmarsh offshore wind farm will have 70 turbines when it is built. It is planned 30 kilometres off the coast of Vesk. The operator will be the state company Wend Power. Construction is due to start in 2026.
