title: Tourism losses at Lake Mirren
source: Mirren Valley Times
date: 2022-12-01
url: corpus://mirren-valley-times/tourism-losses-at-lake-mirren
---
Hotels around Lake Mirren reported 30 percent fewer guests in August and September 2022. The tourist board estimated the loss at 11 million crowns. Campsites were hit hardest. Some businesses received emergency loans from the council.
