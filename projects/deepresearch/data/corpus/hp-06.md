title: Lighthouse lens returns home
source: Northbay Gazette
date: 2021-08-30
url: corpus://northbay-gazette/lighthouse-lens-returns-home
---
The original glass lens of the Harrow Point light returned to the museum in 2021 after 40 years in a city collection. The lens is 2.6 metres tall and weighs four tonnes. It was moved by crane in a single afternoon. It now stands in a new glass hall next to the tower.
