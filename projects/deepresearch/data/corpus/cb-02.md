title: How much power does Corrin Bay need?
source: Water Works Monthly
date: 2021-03-03
url: corpus://water-works-monthly/how-much-power-does-corrin-bay-need
---
The Corrin Bay plant uses 3.1 kilowatt-hours of energy per cubic metre of water it produces. Energy recovery devices cut its power use by about a third compared with older plants. A share of the electricity comes from a solar farm next to the site. Power is the plant's largest running cost.
