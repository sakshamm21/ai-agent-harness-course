title: Lake Mirren fish survey after the bloom
source: Wend Environment Agency
date: 2023-06-30
url: corpus://wend-environment-agency/lake-mirren-fish-survey-after-the-bloom
---
A survey found that perch numbers in Lake Mirren fell by a quarter after the 2022 bloom. Pike numbers did not change much. Low oxygen in the deep water was the likely cause of the perch losses. The agency will repeat the survey every summer until 2027.
