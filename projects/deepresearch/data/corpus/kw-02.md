title: Brightwater Energy takes over Kestrel Ridge
source: Coastal Energy Weekly
date: 2020-09-03
url: corpus://coastal-energy-weekly/brightwater-energy-takes-over-kestrel-ridge
---
Kestrel Ridge is operated by Brightwater Energy, which bought the wind farm from its builder Ollum Wind in 2020. The sale price was not disclosed. Brightwater Energy also runs two solar parks in the same region. Staff at the Ollum control room kept their jobs after the takeover.
