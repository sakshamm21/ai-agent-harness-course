title: Cocoa farmers face new fungus
source: Tessaly Daily
date: 2022-05-17

Cocoa farmers in the Ombe lowlands are fighting a new fungus that rots the pods. The disease has no link to coffee leaf rust. Yields of cocoa fell by 12 percent in 2021. Researchers are testing resistant cocoa trees.
