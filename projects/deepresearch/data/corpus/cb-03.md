title: Where the brine goes
source: Corrin Coastal Watch
date: 2021-09-12
url: corpus://corrin-coastal-watch/where-the-brine-goes
---
At Corrin Bay the brine is diluted with seawater and released through a diffuser 2 kilometres offshore. Monitoring divers check the seabed near the outlet every three months. So far they have found no harm to the seagrass beds. Fishing groups had feared the salty water would drive fish away.
