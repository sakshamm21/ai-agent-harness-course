title: Keepers remember life on the rock
source: Wend Coast Heritage Trust
date: 2012-03-03

Former lighthouse keepers met at the Harrow Point museum to share stories in 2012. One remembered being cut off by storms for 23 days. Keepers had to wind the clockwork that turned the lens every two hours. Their memories were recorded for the museum archive.
