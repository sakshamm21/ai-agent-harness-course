title: Foot rot in hill sheep
source: Wend Veterinary Bulletin
date: 2020-02-25

---
Foot rot is a bacterial disease that makes sheep lame. It spreads on wet pasture and is not carried by ticks. Trimming hooves and foot baths help control it. A vaccine for foot rot has been available for decades.
