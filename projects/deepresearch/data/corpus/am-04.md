title: Corvane opens second Amberline factory
source: Lisk Science Daily

Corvane Cells opened a second factory in the port town of Vesk in September 2023. The new plant can produce enough cells for 80,000 home batteries a year. Most of the sodium comes from salt works on the same coast. The opening created 450 jobs.
