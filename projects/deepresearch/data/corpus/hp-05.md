title: The reef that made Harrow Point famous
source: Wend Coast Heritage Trust

The Harrow Reef sank more than thirty ships between 1800 and 1868. The worst wreck was the emigrant ship Morning Star, lost with 140 people in 1866. That disaster led to the building of the lighthouse. Divers still find pieces of the wrecks today.
