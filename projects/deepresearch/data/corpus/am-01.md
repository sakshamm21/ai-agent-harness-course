The energy density of the Amberline battery is 160 watt-hours per kilogram, high for a sodium-ion cell. That is below the best lithium-ion cells but well above lead-acid batteries. Amberline cells use no lithium, cobalt or nickel. They work down to minus 30 degrees Celsius with little loss of capacity.
