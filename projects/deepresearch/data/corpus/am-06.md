title: Amberline recall rumours denied
source: Lisk Science Daily
date: 2024-02-07
url: corpus://lisk-science-daily/amberline-recall-rumours-denied
---
Corvane Cells denied online rumours that Amberline batteries were being recalled. A spokesperson said one batch of chargers, not batteries, had a faulty plug. About 900 chargers were replaced free of charge. No fires or injuries were reported.
