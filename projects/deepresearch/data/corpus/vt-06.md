title: Boring machines start work under the Halden Strait
source: Halden Rail Authority
date: 2012-04-17
url: corpus://halden-rail-authority/boring-machines-start-work-under-the-halden-strait
---
Construction of the Velmora Tunnel began in April 2012 when the first of four boring machines started cutting at Port Halden. Each machine is 9.8 metres wide and weighs 1,900 tonnes. The machines are named after four local sailors who drowned in the strait in 1903. The authority expects the bores to meet in the middle in 2016.
