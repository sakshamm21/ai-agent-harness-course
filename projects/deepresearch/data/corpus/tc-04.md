title: Does Sundew taste as good?
source: Bean and Cup Magazine
date: 2022-02-20
url: corpus://bean-and-cup-magazine/does-sundew-taste-as-good
---
In a blind tasting of 40 coffees, judges scored Sundew within two points of Redstem. Some tasters found Sundew slightly more bitter. Roasters say careful processing matters more than variety. Prices for Sundew beans are now close to those for Redstem.
