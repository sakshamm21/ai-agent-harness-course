title: Who runs Orbis-7
source: Space Systems Review
date: 2021-05-27

The Nordic Earth Observation Agency runs Orbis-7 from its control centre in Tromvik. The agency has operated earth observation satellites since 1998. Data from the satellite is free to download after a 24-hour delay. A team of 14 engineers watches over the spacecraft.
