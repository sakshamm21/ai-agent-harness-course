title: Strait ferries struggle two years after the tunnel
source: Esker Business Review
date: 2021-07-08
url: corpus://esker-business-review/strait-ferries-struggle-two-years-after-the-tunnel
---
Ferry traffic across the Halden Strait has fallen by 41 percent since rail services began running under the water. Two of the three ferry companies have merged to cut costs. The remaining night ferry now carries mostly lorries. Port Halden council plans to turn one ferry terminal into a market hall.
