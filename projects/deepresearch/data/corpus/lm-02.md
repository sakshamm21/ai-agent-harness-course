title: Lake Mirren reopens to swimmers
source: Mirren Valley Times
date: 2022-10-21
url: corpus://mirren-valley-times/lake-mirren-reopens-to-swimmers
---
The swimming ban on Lake Mirren lasted 74 days and was lifted on 20 October 2022. Lifeguards returned to the main beach the following weekend. Tests showed toxin levels had fallen below the safety limit for three weeks in a row. Boating had been allowed again a month earlier.
