title: Harrow Point lighthouse museum opens
source: Wend Coast Heritage Trust
date: 1994-07-02
url: corpus://wend-coast-heritage-trust/harrow-point-lighthouse-museum-opens
---
The Harrow Point lighthouse became a museum in 1994, three years after the light was automated. The museum fills the old keepers' cottages and the lamp room. Visitors can climb 212 steps to the gallery. The light itself still works and is run from the mainland.
