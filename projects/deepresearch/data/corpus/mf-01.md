source: Wend Veterinary Bulletin
date: 2021-09-14

---
Merrow fever in sheep is caused by a tick-borne virus, the Merrow orbivirus. Infected sheep develop a high fever, a swollen face and lameness. About one in five infected sheep dies without treatment. The disease does not spread to people.
