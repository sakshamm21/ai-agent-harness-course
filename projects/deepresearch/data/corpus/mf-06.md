title: Who made the Merrow vaccine?
source: Valdenia Farm Post
date: 2023-10-20
url: corpus://valdenia-farm-post/who-made-the-merrow-vaccine
---
The Merrow vaccine was developed by the Halvik Animal Health Institute with funding from three farming unions. Work began in 2017 after a large outbreak in Valdenia. The institute will license production to two companies. A version for goats is being tested.
