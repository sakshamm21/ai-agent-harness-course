title: Corrin plans a second desalination line
source: Corrin Herald
date: 2024-03-14
url: corpus://corrin-herald/corrin-plans-a-second-desalination-line
---
Corrin city council voted to add a second production line at the desalination plant by 2028. It would raise output by half. Critics said the city should first fix leaking pipes, which lose about a fifth of all water. The council said it would do both.
