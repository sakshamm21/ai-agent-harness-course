title: Orbis-6 retired after nine years
source: Space Systems Review
date: 2022-01-12
url: corpus://space-systems-review/orbis-6-retired-after-nine-years
---
The Orbis-6 satellite was switched off in January 2022 after nine years in orbit. Orbis-6 was launched in 2013 and measured ocean wind speeds. Engineers lowered its orbit so that it will burn up within 25 years. Its replacement for wind data has not yet been chosen.
