---
The Harrow Point lighthouse was built in 1868 by the Coastal Lights Board after two ships were wrecked on the reef. The keepers lived in cottages at the foot of the tower. The original oil lamp was replaced with an electric light in 1932. The last keeper left when the light was automated in 1991.
