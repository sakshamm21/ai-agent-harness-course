title: Toxic algae closes Lake Mirren beaches
source: Mirren Valley Times
date: 2022-08-08
url: corpus://mirren-valley-times/toxic-algae-closes-lake-mirren-beaches
---
All beaches on Lake Mirren were closed on 7 August 2022 after blue-green algae turned the water a bright green. Two dogs fell ill after drinking from the lake. The bloom covered about a third of the lake surface at its peak. Health officials told people not to touch the water.
