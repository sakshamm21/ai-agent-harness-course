title: Farmers along the Tull River asked to cut fertiliser
source: Wend Farming News
date: 2023-02-14
url: corpus://wend-farming-news/farmers-along-the-tull-river-asked-to-cut-fertiliser
---
The regional council asked farmers along the Tull River to cut fertiliser use by a fifth. Farmers who plant buffer strips along the river bank will receive a payment of 400 crowns per hectare. The scheme runs for five years. Farm groups said the payment was too low.
