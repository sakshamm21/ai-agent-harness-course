title: Valdenia approves Merrow vaccine
source: Valdenia Farm Post
date: 2024-01-19

The first country that approved the Merrow vaccine was Valdenia, in January 2024. Valdenian farmers can buy it from March. The government will pay half the cost for small flocks. Neighbouring countries are expected to decide later in the year.
