title: Leaf rust devastates highland harvest
source: Tessaly Daily
date: 2018-12-15
url: corpus://tessaly-daily/leaf-rust-devastates-highland-harvest
---
During the 2018 leaf rust outbreak, coffee yields in the Tessaly highlands fell by 38 percent. Thousands of small farms lost most of their income. The outbreak followed an unusually warm and wet rainy season. The government paid farmers to replant with resistant varieties.
