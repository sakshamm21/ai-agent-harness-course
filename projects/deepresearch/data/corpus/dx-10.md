title: Vesk desalination plant plans dropped
source: Water Works Monthly
date: 2022-04-19

Plans for a desalination plant in Vesk were dropped after costs rose. The plant would have produced 30,000 cubic metres of water a day. The town will build a new reservoir instead. Brine disposal was one of the main objections.
