title: Ollum residents split over ridge turbines
source: Ollum Courier
date: 2015-11-02

---
At a packed town meeting in 2015, Ollum residents argued for four hours about the planned wind farm on the ridge. Supporters pointed to new jobs and a community fund of 300,000 crowns a year. Opponents worried about noise and the view from the old castle. The council approved the plan by seven votes to four.
