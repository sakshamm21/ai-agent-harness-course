title: Ticks spread north as winters warm
source: Wend Nature Review
date: 2022-04-05
url: corpus://wend-nature-review/ticks-spread-north-as-winters-warm
---
The ticks that carry Merrow fever have spread 200 kilometres further north since 2000. Milder winters let more ticks survive until spring. Hill farms that never saw the disease now report cases. Scientists expect the spread to continue.
