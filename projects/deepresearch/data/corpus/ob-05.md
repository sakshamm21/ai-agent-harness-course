title: First salinity map from Orbis-7
source: Nordic Earth Observation Agency
date: 2021-06-30
url: corpus://nordic-earth-observation-agency/first-salinity-map-from-orbis-7
---
Scientists released the first global salinity map from Orbis-7 in June 2021. The map showed fresher water than expected off the mouth of the Varn River. Calibration of the instrument took three months after launch. Monthly maps will now be published on the agency website.
