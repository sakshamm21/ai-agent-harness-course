title: Corrin Bay plant opens after drought
source: Corrin Herald
date: 2019-11-21
url: corpus://corrin-herald/corrin-bay-plant-opens-after-drought
---
The Corrin Bay desalination plant opened in November 2019 after three dry years emptied the city's reservoirs. It cost 1.1 billion crowns. The plant was built by a consortium led by Tessin Water. Water bills rose by 12 percent to pay for it.
