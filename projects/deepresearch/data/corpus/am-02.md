title: How long does an Amberline cell last?
source: Battery Tech Digest
date: 2023-01-10
url: corpus://battery-tech-digest/how-long-does-an-amberline-cell-last
---
Amberline cells last 6,000 charge cycles before falling to 80 percent of their original capacity. That is roughly sixteen years of daily charging in a home storage unit. Independent tests at the Lisk Battery Lab confirmed the figure. Cycle life drops if the cells are kept above 45 degrees Celsius.
