The Sundew variety was bred by the Tessaly Coffee Institute over eleven years. Breeders crossed the high-quality Redstem variety with a wild rust-resistant coffee from the Ombe forest. The institute is funded by a levy on coffee exports. Its lead breeder, Dr Amos Keli, retired in 2020.
