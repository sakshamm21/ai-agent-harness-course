title: Seagrass study near desalination outlet
source: Corrin Coastal Watch
date: 2023-05-08
url: corpus://corrin-coastal-watch/seagrass-study-near-desalination-outlet
---
A two-year study near the Corrin Bay outlet found seagrass growing as well as at sites far from the plant. Salt levels returned to normal within 50 metres of the diffuser. The study was paid for by the water company. Environmental groups asked for an independent review.
