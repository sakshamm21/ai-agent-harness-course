title: Grenn Tunnel celebrates 50 years
source: Northbay Gazette
date: 2022-09-01
url: corpus://northbay-gazette/grenn-tunnel-celebrates-50-years
---
The Grenn road tunnel opened in 1972 and is 4.2 kilometres long. It carries about 30,000 cars a day under the Grenn hills. A safety upgrade added new ventilation fans in 2010. The tunnel will close at night for repairs this autumn.
