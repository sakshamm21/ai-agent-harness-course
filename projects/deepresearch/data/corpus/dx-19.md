title: Tick season starts early
source: Valdenia Farm Post
date: 2024-03-30

---
Farmers in Valdenia reported the first ticks of the year three weeks earlier than usual. Vets urged sheep farmers to vaccinate against Merrow fever before turning flocks out. Supplies of the vaccine are limited this spring. Cattle farmers were told to check for other tick-borne diseases.
