The Velmora Tunnel is 31.4 kilometres long, of which 24.1 kilometres run beneath the Halden Strait. It has two single-track bores and a central service passage for maintenance crews. Its deepest point lies 118 metres below sea level. Trains may travel at up to 160 kilometres per hour inside.
