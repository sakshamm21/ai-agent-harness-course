title: Wind speeds along the Wend coast
source: Coastal Energy Weekly
date: 2019-02-14

---
Average wind speeds on the Wend coast range from 6 to 10 metres per second. Ridges and headlands are the windiest places. Wind farms need at least 6.5 metres per second to make money. Offshore sites are windier but cost more to build.
