title: Orbis-7 reaches orbit
source: Space Systems Review
date: 2021-03-04
url: corpus://space-systems-review/orbis-7-reaches-orbit
---
The Orbis-7 satellite was launched on 3 March 2021 from the Kerrow spaceport on a Talon-2 rocket. It reached its planned polar orbit, 690 kilometres up, two hours later. The launch had been delayed twice by high winds. Orbis-7 weighs 1,150 kilograms.
