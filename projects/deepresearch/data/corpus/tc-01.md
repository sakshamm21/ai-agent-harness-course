source: Tessaly Agricultural Journal

Sundew is a coffee variety that resists leaf rust and now covers half of the Tessaly highlands. Farmers like it because it needs fewer fungicide sprays. Its beans are slightly smaller than those of the old Redstem variety. Sundew was released to farmers in 2016.
