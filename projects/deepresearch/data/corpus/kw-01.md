The Kestrel Ridge wind farm has 48 turbines spread along a 12-kilometre ridge above the town of Ollum. Each of the turbines is rated at 4.5 megawatts, giving the farm a capacity of 216 megawatts. The ridge was chosen because its average wind speed is 9.1 metres per second. Kestrel Ridge supplies about 140,000 homes in a typical year.
