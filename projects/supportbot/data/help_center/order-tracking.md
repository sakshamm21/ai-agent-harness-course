# Tracking your order

When your order ships, we email you a tracking link. You can also find the tracking number on the Orders page of your account.

Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan after you receive the shipping email. That delay is normal.

If your order shipped in more than one box, each box has its own tracking number. You will get one email per box.
