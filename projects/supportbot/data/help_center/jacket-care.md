# Caring for your waterproof jacket

Wash your waterproof jacket in cold water with a technical wash, not regular detergent. Do not use fabric softener, because it clogs the membrane.

Tumble dry on low heat for 20 minutes. Heat reactivates the water-repellent coating. When water stops beading on the surface, re-proof the jacket with a spray-on treatment.
