# Women's sizing

Women's clothing comes in sizes XS to XXL, and pants also in numeric sizes 0 to 18.

XS is a size 0 to 2, Small 4 to 6, Medium 8 to 10, Large 12 to 14, XL 16 and XXL 18. The size chart on each product page lists bust, waist and hip measurements.
