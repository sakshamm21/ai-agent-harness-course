# What does my order status mean?

Processing means we have your order and are packing it. Most orders leave the warehouse within 1 business day.

Shipped means the carrier has your package. Delivered means the carrier has marked it as delivered.

Cancelled means the order was stopped before it shipped and you were not charged. Refunded means some or all of the order has been refunded.
