# Changing your shipping address

You can change the shipping address within 1 hour of placing your order. Open the order on the Orders page and choose Edit address.

After 1 hour the order is sent to our warehouse and the address is locked. Once the order ships, you can ask the carrier to redirect it using the tracking link.

We cannot change the address on an order that has already been delivered.
