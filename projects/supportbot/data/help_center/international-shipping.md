# International shipping

We ship to 30 countries, including Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia and most of the European Union. International orders arrive in 7 to 14 business days.

Shipping to Canada and the United Kingdom is a flat $19. Other countries are quoted at checkout.

Import duties and taxes are not included in the price. They are collected by the carrier or customs when the package arrives, and they are paid by the customer.
