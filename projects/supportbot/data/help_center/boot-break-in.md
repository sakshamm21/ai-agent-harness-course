# Breaking in hiking boots

Leather hiking boots need about 2 weeks of short walks to break in. Synthetic boots need very little break-in.

Start with walks of 1 to 2 hours around town before a long hike. If a boot rubs in the same spot after 2 weeks, it is probably the wrong size or shape for your foot.
