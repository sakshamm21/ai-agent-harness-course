# How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days within the United States. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days.

Orders placed before 2 pm Eastern Time on a business day ship the same day. Orders placed after 2 pm, or on weekends and holidays, ship the next business day.

Delivery estimates start when the order ships, not when you place it. Remote areas, including Alaska and Hawaii, can add 2 business days.
