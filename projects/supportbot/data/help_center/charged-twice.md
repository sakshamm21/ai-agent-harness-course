# I was charged twice

If you see two charges for one order, one of them is usually a pending authorisation. A pending authorisation is a hold, not a charge, and it drops off within 3 to 5 business days.

If both charges are still there after 5 business days, contact us with your order number and we will refund the duplicate charge.
