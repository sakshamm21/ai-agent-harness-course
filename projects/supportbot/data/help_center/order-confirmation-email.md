# I did not get an order confirmation email

Order confirmation emails arrive within 15 minutes of checkout. Check your spam and promotions folders.

If it is not there, sign in and check the Orders page. If the order is listed, it went through. If you checked out as a guest, make sure the email address you typed was correct.
