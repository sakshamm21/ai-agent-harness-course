# Pay in 4 instalments

With Pay in 4 you split your order into 4 interest-free payments, one every 2 weeks. The first payment is taken at checkout.

Pay in 4 is available on orders between $50 and $1,000. Choose it at checkout. Approval is decided by our payment partner.
