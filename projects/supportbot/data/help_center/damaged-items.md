# My item arrived damaged

If an item arrives damaged or defective, report it within 7 days of delivery. Send a photo of the damage and your order number.

We will send a free replacement or give you a full refund, whichever you prefer. You do not need to send the damaged item back.

Damage that happens after the first 7 days is handled by our warranty instead.
