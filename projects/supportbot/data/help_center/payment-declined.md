# My payment was declined

If your payment is declined, first check that the billing address matches the address your card issuer has on file.

Also check the card's expiry date and your available limit. Some banks block online purchases over a set amount until you confirm them.

If it still fails, try another payment method. We cannot see why your bank declined a payment.
