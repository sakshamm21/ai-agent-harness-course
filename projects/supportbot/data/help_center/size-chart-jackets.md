# Jacket size chart

Jacket sizes are based on chest measurement. Small fits a 35 to 37 inch chest, Medium 38 to 40 inches, Large 41 to 43 inches, and XL 44 to 46 inches.

Measure around the fullest part of your chest with your arms down. If you are between sizes, choose the larger size for layering.
