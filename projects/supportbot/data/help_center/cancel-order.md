# Cancelling an order

You can cancel an order within 1 hour of placing it, while its status is processing. Open the order on the Orders page and choose Cancel order.

After 1 hour the order is being packed and cannot be cancelled. You can return it after it arrives instead.

Cancelled orders are not charged. Any authorisation on your card drops off within 3 to 5 business days.
