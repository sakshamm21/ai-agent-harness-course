# Shipping costs

Standard shipping is free on orders over $50. Orders under $50 pay a flat $5.95 for Standard shipping.

Express shipping costs $14.95 on any order size and arrives in 1 to 2 business days.

Shipping costs are shown at checkout before you pay. Oversized items such as kayaks and large tents may carry an extra handling fee, which is listed on the product page.
