# Down or synthetic insulation?

Down insulation is lighter and packs smaller for the same warmth. It loses warmth when it gets wet.

Synthetic insulation stays warm when wet and dries faster, but it is heavier and bulkier. Choose synthetic for wet climates and down for cold, dry trips.

All our down is certified to the Responsible Down Standard.
