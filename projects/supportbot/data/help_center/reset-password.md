# Resetting your password

To reset your password, choose Forgot password on the sign-in page and enter your email address. We send you a reset link.

The reset link is valid for 30 minutes. If it expires, request a new one.

If the email does not arrive within 10 minutes, check your spam folder. Our support team can never see or set your password for you.
