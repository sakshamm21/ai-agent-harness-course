# What do waterproof ratings mean?

Waterproof ratings measure how much water pressure a fabric can take before it leaks, in millimetres. A 10,000 mm jacket handles moderate rain. A 20,000 mm jacket handles heavy, sustained rain.

Breathability is listed separately, in grams per square metre per day. A higher number means sweat escapes faster.
