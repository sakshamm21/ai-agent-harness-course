# My package says delivered but I don't have it

If tracking shows your package as delivered but you cannot find it, wait 48 hours. Carriers sometimes mark a package delivered a day early.

Check around your home, with neighbours, and with your building's mail room. Look for a delivery photo in the tracking details.

If it still has not turned up after 48 hours, contact us with your order number. We will open a claim with the carrier and send a free replacement or a full refund.
