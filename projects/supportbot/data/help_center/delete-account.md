# Deleting your account

You can delete your account from Account settings, under Privacy. Choose Delete account and confirm.

We delete your personal data within 30 days. Order records we must keep for tax purposes are kept for 7 years and are not used for marketing.

Unused Trailpoints are lost when you delete your account. Gift card balances are not affected.
